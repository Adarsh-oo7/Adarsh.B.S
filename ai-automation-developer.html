5-Stage AI Engineering & Shadow-Run Methodology

Deploying AI directly to customers without rigorous testing risks brand reputation. Every AI project follows a strict 5-stage engineering protocol with a mandatory shadow-run validation phase.

STAGE 01

Workflow Audit

We map your current manual processes, API endpoints, and data sources to calculate exact time & cost savings before writing code.

STAGE 02

Architecture & NDA

We sign a bilateral NDA, select the optimal LLM/vector stack, and provision secure cloud infrastructure under your ownership.

STAGE 03

Agent & Pipeline Build

Rapid sprint engineering of prompts, RAG embeddings, n8n webhooks, and deterministic fallback guardrails.

STAGE 04

Shadow-Run Testing

The AI runs in parallel ("shadow mode") on real incoming data with human review before it is allowed to reply to live clients.

STAGE 05

Cutover & Handover

Full production launch, monitoring dashboard setup, team training, and 100% repository & documentation handover.
